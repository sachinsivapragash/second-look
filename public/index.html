How your privacy is protected
  1. Instant checks run on your device for the classic tricks: codes, gift cards, pressure, look-alike links.
  2. If Chrome’s built-in Gemini Nano is ready, the AI runs on your device too. Nothing is sent.
  3. If not, phone numbers, emails, card numbers, codes and link paths are removed on your device first, then Gemini checks the rest through Firebase AI Logic. The receipt shows exactly what was sent.
  4. The AI can raise a warning, never lower it below the instant checks, and it ignores instructions hidden in the message.
